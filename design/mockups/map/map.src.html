Roadteller: the map of shaded squares

Three ways to draw where the injury collisions were, with real figures for 2021 to 2025: 250-metre squares within a mile of the middle of RG14 (Newbury), and one-kilometre squares across West Berkshire. Counts only, no rates. Use the dark bar to switch between them. Point at a square, or tap it, for its count. Everything is drawn on the page from Ordnance Survey's open data, with no outside map service.
